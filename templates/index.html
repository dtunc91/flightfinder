How to find cheap flights from your airport

Most flight sites want a destination before they show a price. Here you start with your airport and a date, and we show the cheapest places you can fly to. Sometimes it's Kraków for £29. Sometimes it's somewhere you'd never have thought to search.

Four habits that save the most

  • Fly midweekTuesday and Wednesday departures are often half the price of a Friday.
  • Try nearby airportsA cheap fare from Stansted can beat a dear one from Heathrow, even after the train.
  • Travel lightOn budget airlines a cabin bag can cost more than the flight.
  • Book holidays earlySchool holiday and bank holiday fares only go one way.
Where do the prices come from?

The prices come from live airline fare data, so you see real one-way and return fares rather than "from" prices that disappear when you click. Every result links straight through to book, and searching costs nothing. No sign-up, no fees, no catch.
