About GetMeOutOfHere.Live

A free flight search tool built around one idea: sometimes you just want to go somewhere cheap — and you don't care where.

What we do

GetMeOutOfHere.Live is a flight discovery tool that works differently from most flight search engines. Instead of asking "where do you want to go?", it asks "where are you flying from?" — then shows you every affordable destination you can reach from your airport on your chosen dates.

You pick your home airport, your departure window, and the number of passengers. We do the rest, showing the cheapest fares travellers have recently found from your airport to every destination, cheapest first. No destination required. No endless tab-switching.

How it works

  1. Enter your airport and dates — use the search form on the homepage. We support hundreds of airports across the UK, Europe, North America, Asia, and beyond.
  2. We look up recent fares — we ask Aviasales (through the Travelpayouts partner network) for the cheapest fares its travellers have found recently from your airport, across airlines such as Ryanair, easyJet, British Airways, Wizz Air and Lufthansa. We show fares for your date first, and the nearest date when a destination has none that day.
  3. Results are ranked by price — you see the cheapest destinations first. Narrow them down by price, direct flights only, or same country versus abroad.
  4. Click to book — when you find a fare you like, we send you directly to the airline or booking site to complete your reservation securely. We never handle payment details ourselves.

Why we built this

We built GetMeOutOfHere.Live for the times when you need a break but haven't decided where to go. Maybe you have a long weekend free and want to escape somewhere warm. Maybe you're a flexible traveller hunting for the best value. Maybe you're just curious what's cheap from your airport right now.

Traditional flight search engines require a destination before they show you anything. That means you're constantly opening new tabs, entering the same dates over and over, and manually comparing prices across dozens of routes. We cut through all of that by aggregating everything in one place and letting the price lead the decision.

Where our prices come from

All flight prices on GetMeOutOfHere.Live come from Aviasales via the Travelpayouts partner network. They are the cheapest fares Aviasales travellers have found recently, usually within the last few days, rather than a live booking quote.

That means a fare can change or sell out before you book, just like on the airline's own website. Always click "See flight" to confirm the current price before making plans.

Affiliate disclosure

GetMeOutOfHere.Live is free to use. Links to Aviasales and to our other travel partners (eSIMs, car hire, activities and flight-delay compensation) are affiliate links: we may earn a commission if you book through them. The commission is paid by the provider and does not add anything to your price.

This affiliate relationship does not influence which flights we show or how we rank them. Results are always sorted by price, not by commission rate.

Who runs this site

GetMeOutOfHere.Live is an independent website run from the UK. It isn't owned by an airline or travel agency. We built it because we kept wanting a cheap break without knowing where to go, and no flight site made that easy.

Our travel blog

Alongside the flight search tool, we publish a travel deals blog covering topics like the cheapest routes from major airports, how to find last-minute deals, the best times to book for peak season travel, and destination guides for budget-conscious travellers. Every guide shows when it was last updated, and we retire guides once they're out of date. Bookmark the blog or subscribe for alerts to stay up to date.
